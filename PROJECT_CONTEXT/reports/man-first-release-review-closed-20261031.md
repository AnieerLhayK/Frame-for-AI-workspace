### Prioritized optimization follow-up

1. Establish paired effectiveness evaluation first. Compare the same Chinese and
   English inputs with and without MAN under matched model/task conditions; blind
   review meaning fidelity, naturalness, necessary information and task fit. Record
   improvement, no improvement and regression. Engineering checks, synthetic
   behavioral trials and paired effectiveness evaluation are distinct evidence;
   neither shorter text nor an AI-detector score establishes better writing.
2. Expand on-demand cases with both repairs and protected counterexamples:
   opaque professional labels, mechanical paragraphs, flattering summaries and
   empty endings versus necessary terms, useful lists and purposeful literary
   repetition. Preserve provenance, privacy review and context limits; do not
   turn the collection into a phrase blacklist.
3. Study paragraph-level judgment: whether paragraphs connect, examples advance
   the argument and conclusions follow from evidence. A sentence form or keyword
   alone is insufficient grounds for an edit.
4. Research automated checks only as a later optional aid. Establish Chinese
   false-positive behavior first; report candidate locations and reasons while
   allowing justified retention. Do not require a linter for every response.
5. Defer personal voice profiles. Reuse the character system's existing voice
   capabilities; retain the boundaries between full MAN preferences, shared
   expression rules and character-only Doctor diagnosis.
